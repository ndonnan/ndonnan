## 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm an experienced Solutions Architect who has worked on large enterprise projects across a number of industries in South Africa, Ireland, and remotely for US and African based clients. Having spent significant time with both startups and large organisations, I enjoy empowering feature teams and building pragmatic, tailored solutions that help drive clients' businesses forward.

I believe in continuous learning. I'm an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional, a Claude Certified Architect, and hold an MSc in Enterprise Architecture Management, which helps me keep technology and business strategy aligned on the projects I work on.

- 🌱 I'm currently learning **how to design and deliver production-grade AI solutions with Claude**
